How We Work

Six steps to clean solar energy —
no confusion, no pressure.

Every project follows a clear, organized process so you always know what's happening next.

01 Step 01

Site Visit & Load Study

We begin with a proper survey of your roof, electrical load, and usage patterns to plan your system accurately.

02 Step 02

Package Recommendation

Based on your needs and budget, we suggest the best-fit solar package with a clear breakdown of costs and benefits.

03 Step 03

Subsidy & Approvals

We handle documentation, government subsidy applications, and approval paperwork so the process feels smooth and simple.

04 Step 04

Professional Installation

Our trained team installs your system using quality components with careful setup, alignment, and a clean professional finish.

05 Step 05

Grid Connection & Metering

We guide you through grid integration, net metering registration, and meter updates for seamless power flow and bill savings.

06 Step 06

Activation & Ongoing Support

Your system goes live and starts reducing bills while we remain available for maintenance, checks, and service support.
